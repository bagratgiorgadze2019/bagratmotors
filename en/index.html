About us

Bagrat Motors is also known as Bagrat Evakuatori — one call gets you towing, mobile tyre service and a mini-excavator.

Our services include 24/7 tow-truck call-out across Georgia, mobile tyre service, and mini-excavator work — digging, foundation excavation, sewerage, water-supply installation, post-hole drilling and pipe welding.

Bagrat Motors brings together three roadside assistance services in one place — towing, mobile tyre service and mini-excavator. Our goal is that a single call gets you the help you need.

The company is led by Bagrat Giorgadze. We work 24/7, aim to be on site within 20–30 minutes and get the job done quickly and to a high standard.
