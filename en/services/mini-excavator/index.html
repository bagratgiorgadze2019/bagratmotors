What work we do

Bagrat Motors' mini-excavator is ideal for city conditions — it fits into narrow yards, does not damage the site and gets through digging work quickly.

  • ✓ Trench digging (water, sewer, cable)
  • ✓ Foundation excavation
  • ✓ Site levelling and grading
  • ✓ Soil excavation and loading
  • ✓ Small-scale demolition in tight spaces
  • ✓ Landscaping and garden works

Why a mini-excavator

  • Compact: fits into narrow passages and yards where larger equipment cannot.
  • Fast: work that would take days by hand is done in hours.
  • Precise: minimal damage to the surrounding area.

Frequently asked questions

What work can the mini-excavator do?+

Digging trenches, foundations and utility lines, site levelling, loading soil and small-scale demolition.

Yes, the mini-excavator is compact and works in tight spaces where larger equipment cannot fit.
