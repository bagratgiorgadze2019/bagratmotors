TPMS sensor replacement and programming

Tire pressure light won't go off? The pressure sensor may be damaged or its battery dead. We check, replace and program it.

Why the light is on

Often the cause is low pressure, so check the pressure first. If it's fine, the sensor or its programming is the likely problem.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do it on any car?+

Not always — it depends on the make, model and year.

What is a TPMS sensor?+

A sensor in the wheel that measures tire pressure and sends it wirelessly to the car — in Georgia it's often called the tire “bluetooth”.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the car and the sensor — call us.
